# Hi, I'm Liam Aljundi

Creative technologist and innovation strategist based in Gothenburg, Sweden. I help
startups and innovators turn ambitious ideas into successful products by refining the
user journey and ensuring adoption, from concept to launch.

My background is in interaction design, cognitive science and user research. I worked at
Arduino as a user researcher and content manager, and I'm now studying for an MSc in
Entrepreneurship and Business Design at Chalmers University of Technology.

## What I do

- **Vision and strategy:** defining what makes a product matter and aligning the whole
  product journey around it, with sprint workshops that keep teams moving.
- **Usability and adoption:** designing with care for real people, using cognitive
  science and iterative testing, so products aren't just used but loved.
- **Integration and aesthetics:** bridging physical and digital with thoughtful, simple
  and desirable design that feels natural to use.

## Experiments

- [Arecibo 9 Mission](https://github.com/LiamAljundi/Arecibo9-Mission): a sci-fi interactive
  fiction game with an audio decoder puzzle, made for an essay on overcoming the lack of
  choices in interactive fiction. [Play Arecibo 9 Mission](https://liamaljundi.github.io/Arecibo9-Mission/start.html).
- [Rakeful](https://github.com/LiamAljundi/Rakeful): a digital Zen garden where you rake
  patterns into sand particles. [Try Rakeful](https://liamaljundi.github.io/Rakeful/).
- [bike-touch-navigation](https://github.com/LiamAljundi/bike-touch-navigation): a bike
  handlebar that guides the rider through touch, using servo motors.
- [camera-vision](https://github.com/LiamAljundi/camera-vision): an artistic piece about
  the relationship between spaces and people, using camera-based face tracking.
- [light-interaction](https://github.com/LiamAljundi/light-interaction): interactive light
  objects built with Arduino and websockets, exploring focus while studying.
